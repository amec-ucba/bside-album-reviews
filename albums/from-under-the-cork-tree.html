Classic Review

From Under the Cork Tree

Fall Out Boy

Island Records · May 2005

8.7 Our score Recommended

From Under the Cork Tree was Fall Out Boy's major-label debut, and it turned a Chicago-area pop-punk band into one of the biggest rock acts of the decade. Patrick Stump wrote the music and Pete Wentz wrote the lyrics, a split that gives the album its signature mix of soaring hooks and sharp, oversharing words.

Song titles you can't fit on a T-shirt, choruses you can't get out of your head.

Then there are the titles. "Our Lawyer Made Us Change the Name of This Song So We Wouldn't Get Sued" opens the album, and it only gets longer from there. Behind the jokes, though, "Sugar, We're Goin Down" and "Dance, Dance" are about as tight as mid-2000s rock singles get, and both became Top 10 hits.
